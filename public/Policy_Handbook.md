# Clause Mind Financial Services — Policy Handbook

**Version:** 2025.3  
**Last updated:** 2025-09-15  
**Audience:** Internal compliance, IT, risk, and data protection teams

This mock handbook mirrors themes commonly required of regulated Indian financial entities under RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, and the DPDP Act, 2023. It is intentionally incomplete in places so the Compliance Copilot can demonstrate gap detection.

---

## POL-ISP — Information Security Policy

**Owner:** CISO Office

### ISP-4.1 Access Control
Access to information systems shall be granted on a need-to-know basis. Privileged access reviews shall be conducted periodically by system owners.

### ISP-5.1 Cryptography
Sensitive customer and transaction data shall be protected using industry-standard encryption mechanisms for data at rest and in transit.

### ISP-6.2 Logging & Monitoring
Security events shall be logged and retained for a minimum of 180 days and reviewed by the Security Operations team.

---

## POL-ACP — Access Control Policy

**Owner:** IT Security Team

### ACP-3.2 Privilege Reviews
Access privilege reviews shall be conducted periodically for all production systems holding customer data.

### ACP-4.1 Joiner-Mover-Leaver
User access shall be provisioned and revoked through the approved IAM workflow within 24 hours of HR status change.

---

## POL-DRP — Data Retention Policy

**Owner:** Data Governance

### DRP-2.1 Retention Periods
Customer KYC records shall be retained for eight years after account closure unless a longer period is required by applicable law.

### DRP-3.4 Secure Disposal
Personal data past retention shall be securely deleted or anonymised and disposal evidence retained for audit.

---

## POL-PRV — Privacy Policy

**Owner:** Data Protection Officer

### PRV-2.1 Purpose Limitation
Personal data shall be processed only for purposes disclosed to the data principal at the time of collection, consistent with DPDP Act 2023 principles.

### PRV-4.2 Cross-border Transfer
Cross-border transfer of personal data is permitted only to jurisdictions and processors approved by the DPO and Legal.

---

## POL-VND — Vendor Risk Management Policy

**Owner:** Risk Management Team

### VND-2.3 Vendor Due Diligence
Critical vendors processing customer data shall undergo security due diligence prior to onboarding. Periodic reassessment is encouraged.
